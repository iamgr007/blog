### Good Morning

The day started with a black coffee, thought I could revise the bitcoin paper and end up with more insights but all the energy was spent in taking a perfect "morning grind" snap! It's time that I introspect my priorities lol.

On the way to office, I listened to this [Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6X6asJuzfWGFyxSA50Ql85?si=8f4fef54e6634d66) 'The Personal and Professional Power of Emotional Awareness' from 'Think fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques' with the guest Celine Teoh and here are the key takeaways:

* Practice asking this question: `Do you want an advice or my support?` - this way we can actually connect more with people and get rid of the habit of listening to someone and just give an advice, you know the implications of that!
* Start/end a meeting by asking people to share a word(a feeling) and a sentence on why are feeling that way. A great way to quickly get a perception on your teammate and understand them more to colloaborate effectively.
* Start a "celebrations" channel on slack where the team comes together to celebrate little things, a small boost to brighten the day!

This podcast helped me understand more about the interpersonal dynamics, gave good insights on how to practice and hone those skills in real life. Looking forward to implement them from today. Started a slack channel, it's up and active! easiest of all! 
I reckon podcast is a better way to quickly get a grip on new concepts, get more insights faster than reading. Also, helps me improve my listening skills - improving my attention span, stop watching shitty Instagram reels and youtube shorts, time to focus!
And blogging helps me to contemplate and jot down all my thoughts, started today again and hope not to break the streak this time!
